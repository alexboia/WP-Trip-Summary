# Every trip has a story. Give it a route.

English canonical campaign, 21 October 2026. Product: WP Trip Summary 0.3.3, source commit `513be0b0d2ff766dfbfe486b5889e52b1ab13c6f`. Audience: WordPress travel writers; a separate companion card addresses developers.

## Promise and proof

Make the route part of the story: add a GPS track to a WordPress post and give readers a map and elevation profile. Supported by `readme/sections/features.md` and the supplied editor/viewer captures in `assets/en_US`. Personal trip media is atmospheric context; the demo track is not represented as the route in the photographs or footage. No invented trip statistics, import-speed claim or compatibility claim.

## Visual system

Navy `#0A3868`, mint `#8CF5D9`, warm paper `#F3F3E9`. Space Grotesk Bold headings, IBM Plex Sans Regular body. Existing logo retained. Real media retains its color; framing, typography and movement provide the treatment. No AI-generated route or product UI. Registry `line-by-line-slide` adapted for headline scale and palette. Ease vocabulary: `power3.out` for lines, `power2.out` for product panels, `sine.inOut` for the illustrative cursor. No audio-reactive decoration: the route remains the focus.

Film, poster, standard/retina WordPress banners, landscape and square social cards, README hero, developer companion card, English share copy with optional Romanian alternative, editable sources and credits. Static exports are staged together in `marketing-assets/`; original source assets and the previous film remain available.
