# Photographs to look for

Canonical English version. The [Romanian alternative](photo-brief.ro.md) is available for the author's convenience; English takes precedence if the versions differ.

Photographs from your own trips are a strong source: they show why the product exists and give it a personal identity. Start with **8–12 photographs**, ideally including **4–6 from the same trip**. They do not need to cover every category below, and a new photo shoot is not required.

| What to look for | Useful composition | Where to use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2–3 wide shots of a road, trail or valley | The route leads the eye; a relatively clear area for a headline; room for a panoramic crop | Banner, film opening, article image |
| 2 shots of a bicycle / someone on the route | Bicycle or person in context, ideally leaving space around the subject | Help viewers recognize the intended user and trip type |
| 1–2 hiking shots | Trail, waymark, ridge or distinctive landmark | An example showing that the plugin also covers walking |
| 1–2 railway shots, if available | Train in a landscape, station or a view from the journey; ideally tied to a route | Dedicated material for railway enthusiasts |
| 1–2 details from the trip | Signpost, road surface, equipment or rest stop | Connect structured information to the actual experience |
| A recognizable beginning and ending | Two images from the same trip that tell a story | Case study and closing sequence |

For a banner, a landscape photograph that can support a very wide crop, approximately 3.1:1, is useful. Start with 16:9 for video; original portrait shots help with a 9:16 version. As a working preference, look for originals at least 2500–3000 px on the long edge if available; smaller photographs may still suit other uses.

Provide originals without text overlays or the compression introduced by screenshots from messaging apps. RAW is not required. Leave final cropping until we know the intended asset: calm shots with open space can be as useful as spectacular panoramas.

## The trip connecting the materials

If possible, choose a trip for which you have photographs, an article and the original GPX file. Briefly note:

- the trip's name, region, type and approximate date;
- which images and GPS file belong to it;
- the article link, if public;
- information entered manually, such as difficulty or access details;
- the photographer and any publication restrictions or required credit.

You do not need to calculate statistics for marketing: verify them against the appropriate source and in the product. A photograph from another trip can provide atmosphere without claiming it depicts the demonstrated route. If no GPX is available, photographic materials can still be prepared while a separately identified demo route is selected.

Before export, establish what can be public: identifiable people, a home starting point or other sensitive locations. Retain originals; remove GPS metadata from public derivatives when unnecessary, and use only the route segment intended for demonstration. Personal photographs should not automatically enter Git or the plugin package.

## Practical selection

Assess each candidate for its intended role: relevance to the trip, clarity, crop options, space for text and permission to use it. Select one main photograph and two crop alternatives. Record the final selection in the campaign's asset register, including the original, associated trip, credit and derived exports.
